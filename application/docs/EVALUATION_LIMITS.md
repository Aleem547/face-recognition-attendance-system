# Evaluation boundaries

Software regression tests use isolated synthetic records, mocked browser/camera interactions or synthetic feature vectors. They establish behavior under those fixtures, not recognition accuracy, liveness, real camera provenance or operational reliability across participants.

The earlier identity diagnostic and NUAA print diagnostic remain separate evidence with their original reports and limitations. Do not convert ranking metrics into an accuracy claim, pool correlated frames as independent presentations, ignore processing failures, or infer screen/replay resistance from a print-only diagnostic. Model pretraining overlap and population/camera coverage remain unresolved where previously documented.

Own-face phone-photo and replay pilots contain varying experimental scores and documented label/order issues. Preserve original exports, including failures. Their score distributions do not establish a validated operating rule. A setting chosen to separate a small pilot must not then be reported as independently evaluated on that same pilot.

The latest motion screenshot shows incomplete attempts with zero completed turns. Its detailed export was not recovered before the results tab was closed; subsequent uploaded exports belonged to earlier sessions. The activity is closed, with the outcome recorded as timed out/unverified. There is no successful genuine-user sequence established by that evidence.

Moved photos, adaptive replay, face substitution and camera injection remain limitations of the motion prototype. Even successful prompt following would not by itself prove liveness or identity continuity. No result from the standalone tool authorizes the attendance app.

Before enabling camera-based automatic marking, the project still needs an explicit decision policy, representative genuine-user and attack testing, development-only calibration, untouched evaluation data and an error-budget assessment. The new experimental automatic code path allows controlled tests under an explicit staff-selected policy, with staff correction available. It does not complete this validation work or establish suitability for institutional attendance.
