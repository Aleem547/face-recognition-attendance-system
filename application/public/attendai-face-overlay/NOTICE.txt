Face Recognition Attendance System camera positioning overlay

Preview frames are processed in a same-origin browser worker. No additional
image upload, face enrollment, identification request, or camera stream is
started by this overlay. The label comes from the signed-in student's existing
authorized account record. A detection box is only a visual positioning guide;
it is not a recognition, identity, liveness, PAD, or attendance decision.

JSFeat and the bundled frontal-face cascade are vendored from:
https://github.com/inspirit/jsfeat/tree/4c7b336bbeeb26e6cd4cdf3c7d414abe273846f3
